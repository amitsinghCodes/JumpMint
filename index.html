Playing games, then making them.

JumpMint started with playing games and wondering how they were made. Every run, every level and every lucky jump teaches something about what makes a game feel good.

Those lessons go straight into the apps and games made here: small ideas, built carefully, that grow into bigger adventures. The channel shows the playing; the apps show what came of it.
