WELCOME TO ALDER VALE

There’s work to do.
And people counting on you.

Tamsin needs stone for the old yard. Ivo has a cutting shed to reopen. Nell wants to finish the reading-room steps. Start with two trucks and a few small deliveries, then help their valley grow.

01

Bring it home.

Collect stone from the quarry, timber from the forest and grain from the farm. Choose the right truck for each load.

02

Make something useful.

Build facilities in Depot Town. Turn raw materials into boards, bread, steel and tools, with a place for every part of the chain.

03

Keep your word.

Deliver what your neighbours need. Follow 45 story jobs through ten chapters, from the loading bench to the Alder crossing.

YOUR YARD, GROWING WITH YOU

A place for
the next idea.

A mill today. A workshop tomorrow. Choose plots, keep supplies moving, and watch a working town take shape beside your depot.

Haul & Harvest is being built as its own Android app. Public download details will appear here when it is ready.

BUILT FOR A QUIETER KIND OF PLAY

The current build plays offline, with no ads, purchases or sign-in. Story scenes have captions and skip controls, and camera motion can be reduced.
