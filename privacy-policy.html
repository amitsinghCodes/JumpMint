Quantum Hop Privacy Policy

Developer on Google Play: Rvasus (JumpMint)

Package: com.rvasusgamingyt.quantumhop

Effective date: 15 October 2026

About this policy

Quantum Hop is published on Google Play by Rvasus under the JumpMint name. This policy applies to Quantum Hop, package com.rvasusgamingyt.quantumhop. RvasusGamingYT is also used in earlier in-game publisher credits. Contact: vasusr76@gmail.com.

Local progress and settings

Quantum Hop stores game progress, scores, unlocked facts and settings in the app's private storage on your device. Rvasus does not operate a server that receives these local records. Android may back up or transfer progress and settings according to your device and account configuration. Google Play Games leaderboards do not provide cloud restoration of your level progress.

Google Play Games

Quantum Hop includes Google Play Games Services for optional online scores. You can play the game without completing Play Games sign-in. Google may automatically authenticate an existing Play Games profile according to your Google account settings. The app reads your Play Games player ID to verify the active player and your display name to show the active profile. When you choose Sync score, the app submits your best-score total for Chapter 1 to Google's leaderboard. Other players' access to your profile and scores depends on your Play Games visibility settings.

Google documents that the Play Games SDK automatically processes gamer identity (gamertag and avatar) when a Play Games account is created or updated, together with service analytics and diagnostic data used to operate and improve its services. Google handles Play Games data under its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Play Games transmits data over encrypted HTTPS connections. Google documents its SDK's data practices at https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection.

Quantum Hop does not use an advertising SDK, in-app purchases or a separate developer analytics SDK. It does not request your Google password, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. We do not sell your personal information.

Crash reports and support email

The app stores a technical crash log locally. Nothing in that log is automatically emailed to Rvasus. If you choose Send report, your email app opens a draft containing the app version, device model, Android version and error details. You can inspect, edit or cancel the draft before sending it. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send. We use this information to respond to you and diagnose problems. Do not include passwords or other information you do not want to share.

We keep support correspondence and crash reports only as long as needed to address the request and any applicable record-keeping obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence by emailing vasusr76@gmail.com.

Your choices and deletion

Settings > Reset Progress clears campaign progress and records on the device. Android's app storage settings can erase all local app data, including settings, any additional game-mode records and local crash logs. Uninstalling also removes local app data, subject to Android backup and restoration. You control backups and device transfers through your Android and Google account settings.

Resetting local progress does not delete scores already submitted to Google Play Games. To change sign-in preferences, profile visibility or delete Quantum Hop's Play Games data, use your Google Play Games profile and data settings. Google's instructions are at https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646. Google controls retention of Play Games data through its account and privacy settings. The game does not automatically resubmit your local scores; Sync score is your choice.

Quantum Hop does not create a separate developer-hosted account. Play Games profiles are managed by Google. Contact vasusr76@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent directly to us.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date when the app's data practices change. The current policy is available at https://amitsinghcodes.github.io/JumpMint/privacy-policy.html.
