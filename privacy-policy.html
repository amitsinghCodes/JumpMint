Haul & Harvest Privacy Policy

JumpMint · package com.jumpmint.haulharvest
Effective date: 17 October 2026 · current development builds

Play locally. No JumpMint account, advertising SDK, in-app purchases or developer analytics service is included in the standalone Haul & Harvest app.

What stays on your device

The app stores your game progress, deliveries, inventory, buildings and settings in private app storage. It also remembers camera positions, story scenes you have seen and your narration preference. Local performance records help diagnose the renderer. The app does not automatically send these records to JumpMint.

Android backup and transfer

Android may back up or transfer your game save and selected settings according to your device and account configuration. Test saves and performance records are excluded by the app's backup rules. JumpMint does not run a cloud-save service. See Android's backup information for how the platform handles backups.

Story narration

Optional narration uses an installed English device voice that Android identifies as available offline. Only the game's written story is passed to the device's text-to-speech engine. If no suitable voice is installed, captions remain available. You can turn the voice off during any story scene. The system voice provider is separate from JumpMint and has its own settings and privacy terms.

Accounts, ads and permissions

This standalone app does not include Google Play Games sign-in or leaderboards, advertising, third-party analytics or purchases. It does not request Internet, location, microphone, camera or contacts access. Following the privacy-policy link opens your browser, where the website's normal hosting and font services apply.

Support and your choices

If you choose to contact vasusr76@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you include, and use it to respond. Do not include passwords or sensitive information. You may ask us to delete support correspondence. To remove local progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall it in Android settings; manage any platform backups separately in your device or account settings.

Changes and scope

This policy describes the standalone Haul & Harvest development build. If the app's data practices change before release, this page will be updated. Quantum Hop is a separate game with the policy below.

Quantum Hop Privacy Policy

Developer on Google Play: Rvasus (JumpMint)

Package: com.rvasusgamingyt.quantumhop

Effective date: 15 October 2026

About this policy

Quantum Hop is published on Google Play by Rvasus under the JumpMint name. This policy applies to Quantum Hop, package com.rvasusgamingyt.quantumhop. RvasusGamingYT is also used in earlier in-game publisher credits. Contact: vasusr76@gmail.com.

Local progress and settings

Quantum Hop stores game progress, scores, unlocked facts and settings in the app's private storage on your device. Rvasus does not operate a server that receives these local records. Android may back up or transfer progress and settings according to your device and account configuration. Google Play Games leaderboards do not provide cloud restoration of your level progress.

Google Play Games

Quantum Hop includes Google Play Games Services for optional online scores. You can play the game without completing Play Games sign-in. Google may automatically authenticate an existing Play Games profile according to your Google account settings. The app reads your Play Games player ID to verify the active player and your display name to show the active profile. When you choose Sync score, the app submits your best-score total for Chapter 1 to Google's leaderboard. Other players' access to your profile and scores depends on your Play Games visibility settings.

Google documents that the Play Games SDK automatically processes gamer identity (gamertag and avatar) when a Play Games account is created or updated, together with service analytics and diagnostic data used to operate and improve its services. Google handles Play Games data under its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Play Games transmits data over encrypted HTTPS connections. Google documents its SDK's data practices at https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection.

Quantum Hop does not use an advertising SDK, in-app purchases or a separate developer analytics SDK. It does not request your Google password, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. We do not sell your personal information.

Crash reports and support email

The app stores a technical crash log locally. Nothing in that log is automatically emailed to Rvasus. If you choose Send report, your email app opens a draft containing the app version, device model, Android version and error details. You can inspect, edit or cancel the draft before sending it. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send. We use this information to respond to you and diagnose problems. Do not include passwords or other information you do not want to share.

We keep support correspondence and crash reports only as long as needed to address the request and any applicable record-keeping obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence by emailing vasusr76@gmail.com.

Your choices and deletion

Settings > Reset Progress clears campaign progress and records on the device. Android's app storage settings can erase all local app data, including settings, any additional game-mode records and local crash logs. Uninstalling also removes local app data, subject to Android backup and restoration. You control backups and device transfers through your Android and Google account settings.

Resetting local progress does not delete scores already submitted to Google Play Games. To change sign-in preferences, profile visibility or delete Quantum Hop's Play Games data, use your Google Play Games profile and data settings. Google's instructions are at https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646. Google controls retention of Play Games data through its account and privacy settings. The game does not automatically resubmit your local scores; Sync score is your choice.

Quantum Hop does not create a separate developer-hosted account. Play Games profiles are managed by Google. Contact vasusr76@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent directly to us.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date when the app's data practices change. The current policy is available at https://amitsinghcodes.github.io/JumpMint/privacy-policy.html.

Map Mela Privacy Policy

Map Mela (मैप मेला), formerly Nakshaa · Developer on Google Play: Rvasus (JumpMint)
Package: com.rvasusgamingyt.nakshaa
Effective date: 24 October 2026 (online play in Khoj from version 1.2.0; Google Play Games leaderboards from version 1.1.0)

Map Mela is a map game about India's states and union territories, made to be suitable for families. Every game works offline. It has no accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics. Two things are optional: from version 1.1.0 you can put your scores on Google Play Games leaderboards, and from version 1.2.0 you can play Khoj online against other players. Those are the only times game information leaves your device.

What stays on your device

So you can continue where you left off, Map Mela saves the following in the app's private storage on your device only: quiz scores and best times, per-state practice history, daily-puzzle results and streaks, earned map styles, the state of an unfinished pass-the-phone game, and settings such as language, sound and vibration. Rvasus does not operate a server that receives any of this. Apart from the leaderboard scores and online matches described below, it stays on your device. Android may back up or transfer this data according to your device and account configuration; see Android's backup information.

Google Play Games leaderboards

From version 1.1.0, Map Mela includes Google Play Games Services for optional leaderboards. You can play every game without signing in to Play Games. Google may sign you in automatically with an existing Play Games profile according to your Google account settings, or ask you to sign in when you tap a leaderboard button.

While you are signed in, the app sends your score to Google after each finished round of a game that has a leaderboard: Khoj, Find the State and Capitals (all 36), Against the Clock, Sab Padosi (Snake), Ulta-Pulta (Mixed), Dakiya Dash, Ghost Run (A to Z) and your Rajya of the Day streak. After you sign in, it also sends the personal bests already saved on your device for those games. Rounds scored with Kids mode's easier rules, friends' challenge codes, practice sets and single-region rounds are not sent. The app does not read your Play Games player ID, name or friends list; Google's own leaderboard screens show your profile and other players' scores. Who can see your profile and scores depends on your Play Games visibility settings.

Google documents that the Play Games SDK processes gamer identity (gamertag and avatar) when a Play Games account is created or updated, the scores a game submits, and service analytics and diagnostic data used to operate and improve its services. Google handles Play Games data under its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Play Games transmits data over encrypted HTTPS connections. Google documents its SDK's data practices at https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection.

Online play (Khoj, from version 1.2.0)

Online play is optional and only starts when you open Play online in Khoj. It uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database), run by Google for Rvasus. When you open it, the app signs in to Firebase anonymously: Firebase gives this installation a random identifier, with no name, email address, phone number or Google account. Your player name, such as "Swift Tiger 42", is made up by the app from that identifier; you never type a name, and there is no chat, so other players see only that name, your team, where you dropped your pins and your points.

To run a match, the app sends to Firebase: your anonymous identifier, a random number the app turns into your player name, your team, whether you are still connected, the room code if you made or joined a room, and for each round where you dropped your pin, its distance, your points and how many clues you used. Firebase stores this in a database hosted by Google in Singapore so that the other players in the match can see it, and processes your device's IP address to connect it. Matches, and their room codes, are deleted automatically about seven days after they start. Nothing from online play is used for advertising or tracking, and it is not combined with your progress on your device. Google's description of how Firebase handles data is at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Sharing results

When you choose to share a result (for example a score line or a picture of your map), the app hands that text or image to the sharing app you pick on your device, such as a messaging app. Nothing is shared unless you choose to, and Rvasus never receives it. The sharing app you choose has its own privacy policy.

Permissions, ads and third parties

Up to version 1.1.0 the app does not itself request the Internet permission; leaderboard scores are sent by Google Play services on your device, and only while you are signed in to Play Games. From version 1.2.0 it requests the Internet and network-state permissions for online play only; every other game still works without a connection. It does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access. On Android 9 and older only, it asks for storage access the first time you save a map postcard to your gallery, and uses it only to write that picture to Pictures/Map Mela; newer Android versions save the picture without this permission. Apart from Google Play Games and, from version 1.2.0, Google Firebase for online play, the app contains no advertising, analytics, social-media or purchase services. We do not sell your personal information. It is distributed through Google Play, which may collect information when you download or update apps as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Following the privacy-policy link from the app opens your browser, where the website's normal hosting and font services apply.

Children

Map Mela is designed for a general audience. It has no accounts of its own and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Google Play Games is optional and is provided by Google under its own account rules; Map Mela sends it only game scores, never names, ages or contact details. Online play is optional too: it uses an anonymous identifier and made-up player names, has no chat and lets players share nothing they type. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Map content

The map of India in Map Mela follows the depiction published by the Survey of India, Government of India. Boundary data is adapted from openly licensed sources; the credits and licences are listed on the app's About screen.

Support and your choices

If you choose to contact vasusr76@gmail.com, we receive your email address and what you include, and use it only to respond. You may ask us to delete that correspondence. To remove all local progress, clear the app's storage or uninstall it in Android settings. Online matches are deleted automatically after about seven days; to have them removed sooner, email us the room code or the date and time you played, and we will delete those matches.

Clearing local progress does not delete scores already sent to Google Play Games. To change sign-in preferences or profile visibility, or to delete Map Mela's Play Games data, use your Google Play Games profile and data settings; Google's instructions are at https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646. Google controls how long it keeps Play Games data through its account and privacy settings.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date before any version of Map Mela changes how it handles information. The current policy is always at https://amitsinghcodes.github.io/JumpMint/privacy-policy.html#nakshaa.
