Privacy Policy

Quantum Hop · Developer: Rvasus

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how Rvasus ("we", "us"), the developer listed on Google Play, handles information in the games and apps we publish on Google Play (together, "the apps"). It applies to every version of the apps we distribute, including:

  • Quantum Hop (Google Play package name: com.rvasusgamingyt.quantumhop)

Rvasus publishes games under the JumpMint name, and this website is JumpMint's home page.

In short: the apps do not collect, sell or share your personal information. Everything they save stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The apps have no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking services. They do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Information stored on your device

So you can continue where you left off, the apps save the following on your device only:

  • Progress, such as completed levels, collected items, best times and medals.
  • Settings, such as sound, music, vibration, controls and display options.

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Crash reports you choose to send

If an app closes unexpectedly, it may offer to send us a crash report by email. Nothing is sent automatically. If you choose to send one, your email app opens with a message containing technical details: the app version, your device model, your Android version and a description of the error. You can read and edit the message before sending it.

We use crash reports only to find and fix bugs, and keep them only as long as needed to do that. When you email us we also receive your email address, which we use only to reply to you. You can ask us to delete any email you have sent us.

Third-party services

The apps are distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update apps, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. The apps themselves contain no third-party advertising, analytics or social-media services.

Children

The apps are suitable for a general audience. Because they collect no personal information, they do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Progress and settings are kept in each app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Because no personal information leaves your device through the apps, we hold none that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of an app changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete an email you sent us: vasusr76@gmail.com
